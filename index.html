CutTheClips Academy

Start with
the camera.
Finish with a vision.

You don't need expensive gear or years of experience. Start with the fundamentals and build your eye, technique and confidence step by step.

01

Photography Fundamentals

Camera basics, types of cameras, lenses, focal length, megapixels, sensors and basic gear.

02

Exposure Triangle

Aperture, shutter speed and ISO. Learn how these three controls change your image.

03

Composition & Framing

Rule of thirds, leading lines, symmetry, negative space, perspective and visual balance.

04

Light & Portraits

Natural light, golden hour, shadows, reflectors, basic artificial lighting and posing.

05

Intermediate Photography

Manual focus, RAW, white balance, depth of field, flash photography, low light and creative techniques.

06

Editing & Final Look

Lightroom basics, colour correction, exposure correction, cropping, masking and developing your own style.
